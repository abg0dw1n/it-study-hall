The IT Study Hall

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

The IT Study Hall is a private, invite-only learning site for IT certifications, with study guides, practice exams, timed mock exams and labs, run by Andrew Godwin. This page explains what data the site uses and why.

What we collect

  • Your email address, used to sign you in. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your email address and basic profile (such as your name) from that provider. We don't receive your password.
  • Your practice progress: answers, flagged questions, exam scores and settings, so your progress follows you between devices.
  • Your display name and comments, if you choose a display name and post in a question discussion.
  • Reports you send about questions or comments.

How it's used

Only to let you sign in, to save and sync your progress, and to run question discussions. We don't sell your data, show ads, use it for marketing, or share it with anyone except the services that run the site.

Where it's stored

Sign-in and progress are handled by Supabase (hosted in the United States). The website is hosted by Vercel. Sign-in emails are sent through Gmail. Your progress is also kept in your browser's local storage so the site works offline; signing out removes it from that browser.

Who can see it

Your progress is protected so that only you can read or change it. Comments are visible to other signed-in users together with your display name. Your email address is never shown to other users. The site owner can see the list of invited email addresses and can review and remove comments and reports.

Community guidelines for discussions

Discussions are for learning. By posting a comment you agree to these rules:

  • Discuss concepts and reasoning. Don't post real exam questions, answers or any other content from a live certification exam, and don't share material you aren't allowed to share. Certification programs prohibit this, and such comments will be removed.
  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats, spam or advertising.
  • Protect privacy. Don't post personal information about yourself or anyone else, and don't post links.
  • Only post your own words. Don't copy text you don't have the right to share.

You keep ownership of what you write, and you give the site permission to store and display it to other signed-in users while it is posted. Comments are provided by users and aren't checked for accuracy, so verify answers against official documentation. The site owner may remove any comment or limit commenting at any time. Use the Report button on a comment to flag a problem. To report content that infringes your rights or to request removal, email andrewbgodwin@gmail.com with the question, the comment and what you'd like done.

Deleting your data

You can edit or delete your own comments at any time. To have your account, progress and comments deleted, email andrewbgodwin@gmail.com. It will be removed within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
